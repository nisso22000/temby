## 2 Purpose and independent businesses

The Provider supplies access to hosted shopping companion and order-management software, including the proxy and administration tools expressly activated in Schedule A. The Operator independently supplies its purchasing and fulfillment service to customers who select it.

The parties are independent contractors. This agreement creates no employment, joint venture, partnership, general agency or authority to bind the other party. The arrangement is nonexclusive. The Provider may serve other operators, and the Operator may use other software.

These provisions describe the agreed conduct. They do not override mandatory law or the parties' actual acts.

## 3 Software access and ownership

Subject to this agreement, the Provider grants the Operator a limited, nonexclusive, nontransferable right during the term to use the activated hosted functions for its own lawful business and to enable its authorized personnel to do so. No source-code delivery, sublicense or ownership transfer is included.

The Provider retains rights in its original software, documentation and TEMBY identity. Third-party software remains subject to its own licences; this agreement grants no rights the Provider does not hold. The Operator retains its own business identity and materials. Personal information remains subject to applicable law and data-subject rights rather than an unrestricted ownership claim by either party.

The Operator must not resell platform access, share access with another business, bypass access controls, access other operators' records, or remove required notices. Any statutory rights that cannot lawfully be restricted remain unaffected.

## 4 Provider role and limits of authority

The Provider manages the technical infrastructure specified in Schedule A, which may be shared across operators. Domain ownership, hosting accounts and infrastructure payments do not authorize the Provider to make purchasing commitments for the Operator.

The Provider does not under this agreement receive or hold customer purchase funds, supply or exchange foreign currency, select currency suppliers, fund foreign purchases, place merchant orders, provide purchasing cards, import or deliver goods, decide customer refunds, or guarantee the Operator's performance.

The Provider may implement the Operator's written pricing instructions, present quotes, create order records, and technically initiate payment requests using an integration approved for the Operator. This limited technical permission does not authorize the Provider to change commercial terms or move customer funds to the Provider. Any materially broader role requires a separate written agreement and prior compliance review.

The Provider remains responsible for its own applicable legal duties and conduct, including technical and data-security duties that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 5 Operator responsibilities

The Operator is responsible for the customer purchasing agreement; lawful pricing and fees; approved payment acceptance; lawful foreign-currency sourcing and merchant payments; purchasing; applicable import and customs formalities; delivery; product-related claims; customer communications; cancellations; refunds; reconciliation; and required business, tax and transaction records.

The Operator approves in writing its conversion inputs, fees, rounding rules, quote validity, price-change policy and refund policy before use. It must not describe an illustrative conversion input as an authorized currency-exchange service unless lawfully entitled to do so. Automatic software processing does not remove the need for lawful rules and reliable validation.

Before payment, the customer must be shown the Operator's legal identity, relevant contact details, the service being purchased, the amount and disclosed fees, the applicable purchasing terms, and who handles fulfillment and refunds. The payment beneficiary must be consistent with the approved arrangement. Neither party may misrepresent merchant affiliation, payment-provider approval or government endorsement.

The Operator must not silently add charges, replace products or alter a paid order. Additional payment or material changes require the customer's agreement. Paid orders retain an identifiable snapshot; subsequent changes are recorded separately. The Operator must implement actual payment-provider refunds and reconcile them rather than merely marking a database entry refunded.

## 6 Compliance evidence and launch condition

Before live payments, the Operator must provide accurate registration and representative information, details of its payment arrangement and approval scope, its purchasing and foreign-currency funding method, applicable authorizations or supporting evidence, customer terms, and a workable refund procedure. It must promptly provide reasonably requested updates and notify the Provider of material changes, restrictions, investigations or revoked permissions affecting the service.

The parties must obtain a documented review by an Algerian lawyer competent in the relevant e-commerce, foreign-exchange, payments and import issues. The review must cover the actual proposed operating model and Provider role, customer disclosures, applicable hosting and domain requirements, and personal-data arrangements. Requirements identified for lawful launch must be completed and recorded before activation.

The Provider shall separately verify its own business and tax status and comply with its applicable duties. Neither the Operator's assurances nor legal advice constitute a guarantee against enforcement. No party may knowingly use or supply the platform to assist unlawful transactions. Unresolved material concerns prevent activation of the affected functions.

Each party must preserve accurate relevant records and cooperate with lawful requests. Neither may require the other to conceal transactions, falsify documents, delete evidence or mislead a customer, bank or authority.

## 7 Customer funds and software fees

Customer purchase payments settle directly to the Operator through the arrangement identified in Schedule A. This agreement grants the Provider no custody of those funds, access to purchasing-card details or authority to withdraw from the Operator's accounts. Platform fees are paid separately by the Operator against invoices issued under the Provider's lawful business status.

The commercial fee is completed in Schedule A. Unless replaced by an expressly signed alternative, the royalty base consists solely of disclosed Operator service fees actually received on eligible orders through the platform, excluding merchandise amounts, delivery, payment-processing charges, taxes separately collected, and refunded or cancelled service fees. A partial refund reduces the royalty base by the actual service-fee portion refunded, not an unrelated merchandise amount.

An order is eligible when the Operator has received its payment and completed the agreed fulfillment service. Later refunds produce a credit in the next statement, with any remaining balance settled on termination. The Operator supplies a monthly statement within ten calendar days after month-end; undisputed invoices are paid within fifteen calendar days of receipt. Genuine discrepancies must be identified promptly with supporting records.

Once per year, and additionally on reasonable evidence of material underreporting, the Provider may arrange a proportionate confidential review of relevant fee records on ten business days' notice. Access must minimize personal information and exclude purchasing credentials. Neither party may change the percentage or fee base unilaterally. Taxes, withholding and invoicing follow applicable law; required withholding evidence must be supplied.

## 8 Hosting support and changes

The Provider pays the infrastructure expenses allocated to it in Schedule A. No uptime percentage, round-the-clock support, response deadline, permanent merchant compatibility, guaranteed account creation, guaranteed cart-detection accuracy or continuing feature development is promised unless expressly specified there.

The Provider shall apply reasonable care to the agreed hosting and security functions, communicate material known incidents, and follow the agreed backup and recovery arrangements. These duties are distinct from unlimited maintenance. New development, compatibility repairs and extensive support require a separate written quotation unless included in Schedule A.

If no acceptable repair can be agreed, the Provider may suspend affected functions or terminate on the notice below. It may not continue presenting known unreliable amounts as validated payable quotes. Payment acceptance must fail closed where material amount or order validation is uncertain. The Operator remains responsible for its existing customer obligations.

## 9 Data security and confidentiality

Before live personal-data processing, the parties must complete Schedule A with their legally appropriate data roles, purposes, categories, locations, permitted hosting providers, access controls, retention, export/deletion arrangements, backup rules and incident contacts. Where required, they must execute a separate data-processing annex. Contract labels do not determine statutory roles.

The Provider may process order information only for authorized platform delivery, security, reconciliation and lawful recordkeeping. It shall implement proportionate safeguards including access separation between operators, restricted administrative access and secure handling of secrets. The Operator manages its authorized staff, account security and lawful customer notices and instructions. Both parties must promptly inform the other of relevant suspected breaches and meet mandatory notification duties.

No customer password may be silently captured. Account-credential handoff is outside this agreement's initial scope and requires a separate approved design, lawful basis, customer disclosure, secure storage and access/deletion controls. Customer credentials and card secrets must not appear in routine logs, payment metadata or promotional messages.

Each party must protect the other's nonpublic business and technical information, limiting use and disclosure to legitimate performance, advisers under confidentiality, or lawful obligations. Where lawful, the other party must receive prior notice of compelled disclosure. These restrictions do not prohibit lawful reporting or cooperation with authorities.

## 10 Brand and advertising permission

The Provider grants limited, revocable, nonexclusive permission during the term to use approved TEMBY names, logos and links solely to describe and promote the Operator's authorized use of the platform. No independent franchise, exclusive territory, brand ownership or right to hold itself out as the Provider is granted.

The Operator may promote its dedicated platform page on social media. Statements must accurately identify it as the purchasing and fulfillment business, avoid implying that TEMBY guarantees purchases or refunds, and comply with written brand guidelines. Proposed material materially describing the Provider's commitments requires its written approval. The Provider must likewise identify the Operator accurately and obtain permission for its marks.

## 11 Operator indemnity and claim handling

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Operator shall indemnify the Provider against documented third-party claims, resulting lawful civil liabilities and reasonable external legal defence costs to the extent caused by the Operator's unlawful purchasing, funding, payment, import or fulfillment conduct; its breach of this agreement; misleading advertising; or failure to perform its customer obligations.

This indemnity excludes the portion caused by the Provider's own breach, fault or unlawful conduct and does not transfer criminal responsibility, obstruct an investigation, bind claimants or authorities, or cover any sanction or payment that law prohibits indemnifying. It is a contractual right to reimbursement, not immunity, and depends on enforceability and the Operator's ability to pay.

The Provider shall notify the Operator promptly, provide reasonable cooperation, and retain independent counsel where conflicts or regulatory/criminal issues arise. Late notice reduces recovery only to the extent of proven resulting prejudice. The Operator may not settle a claim in a way admitting the Provider's fault, imposing nonmonetary obligations on it or restricting its lawful defence without written consent. Reasonable documented defence costs attributable to covered civil claims shall be reimbursed as incurred, subject to final adjustment.

No limitation excludes fraud, intentional misconduct, gross negligence where exclusion is prohibited, or any other responsibility that cannot lawfully be limited. These provisions do not restrict customer or authority rights. The cap, exclusions and indemnity must be specifically reviewed for enforceability before signature.

## 13 Suspension term and termination

The agreement starts on the effective date and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate on thirty calendar days' written notice. A material remediable breach may be terminated if not cured within fifteen calendar days after written notice.

The Provider may immediately suspend affected new orders, payments or access where reasonably necessary for security, suspected unlawful use, missing required approvals, a lawful authority/provider direction, or serious customer harm. It shall give reasons and notice where lawful and practicable, keep the action proportionate, preserve evidence and review reinstatement when the issue is resolved. No duty to continue a potentially unlawful service arises.

Suspension or termination does not release the Operator's existing purchasing, refund or recordkeeping obligations. The parties shall arrange a secure export of necessary Operator order records within thirty days where lawful. The Provider is not required to keep accepting new orders or take over fulfillment. Retention and deletion follow Schedule A, statutory duties and legal holds. Lawful access/export necessary for outstanding orders must not be withheld merely to pressure payment.

Accrued fees, valid credits, confidentiality, applicable retention duties, ownership, claim provisions and dispute provisions survive as necessary.
