## 1 Permission and ownership

The Provider grants the Operator nonexclusive, nontransferable access to its hosted TEMBY software for the Operator's lawful purchasing business. The Provider retains its software rights; no source code or ownership is transferred. Third-party components remain subject to their own licences. Both parties remain independent businesses; neither may bind the other. This agreement creates no employment, partnership or general agency.

The Provider manages the hosting and infrastructure at its expense, except separately agreed charges below. The Operator is responsible for its authorized staff and account security. The Provider must use reasonable care in delivering the agreed technical service, safeguarding data and separating operators' access, and must comply with its own mandatory legal duties.

## 2 Purchasing business and customer money

The Operator independently provides the purchasing service and is responsible for its lawful currency funding, payment acceptance, purchases, import formalities where applicable, delivery, customer support, cancellations, refunds and required records. It approves its pricing rules and customer terms. It must disclose all fees and obtain customer agreement before additional charges or material changes to a paid order.

Customer payments settle directly to the Operator through its approved merchant arrangement. The Provider does not hold customer purchase funds, supply or exchange currency, place merchant purchases, or undertake fulfillment. The software may calculate quotes, record orders and technically request payments under the Operator's approved instructions. This does not authorize the Provider to make other commercial commitments.

The website, checkout, payment flow and advertising must clearly identify the Operator as the business providing purchasing and fulfillment and give its customer contact details. Neither party may falsely claim merchant affiliation or regulatory approval. These statements must match actual conduct.
